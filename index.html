INSTALLATION

Get started with HForked.

Follow these steps to install the launcher and prepare Minecraft 26.3.

  1. Download the hforked-launcher.zip archive.
  2. Open the archive using an archiver such as 7-Zip or WinRAR.
  3. Extract HForked Launcher.exe to a convenient location, such as your Desktop.
  4. Extract the Minecraft 26.3 folder from the archive to a preferred location on your computer.
  5. Launch the application by running HForked Launcher.exe.
  6. Open account management in the top-right corner. The default profile is player. To change it, select Manage accounts, enter your desired username in Offline name, and click Add. The new profile will be selected automatically.
  7. Open Versions at the top, select version 0.2.0 or newer, and wait for the download to finish.
  8. Return to Home. Under Minecraft Folder, click Choose and select the location of your 26.3 folder.
  9. Above Minecraft Folder, find Resources and click Prepare.
  10. Wait until all four preparation steps finish, then click Play.
